# ReMatch demo scope

ReMatch demonstrates patient-initiated synthetic screening with one central backend and one role-aware app. A PI/site lists a study with a simulated flat fee. The patient controls record import, matching and each exact introduction. Supported low-risk opportunities reach patients directly; higher or unknown-risk opportunities require a brief usual-doctor discussion. Formal study consent, eligibility and enrollment remain the research site's responsibility.

Run instructions and the judges' story are in [DEMO.md](../DEMO.md). The product authority is [CHANGES.md](../CHANGES.md). The pre-pivot PLAN.md is historical; the old screenshots, referral walkthrough, separate role apps and packaged old demo are not submission artifacts for this version.

Report software events such as discussions created and introductions authorized, not proven clinical impact, enrollment improvement or measured physician time savings. All patients, records, risk fixtures, money, messages, introductions and enrollment outcomes are simulated. Public source retrieval and configured study-text model calls may be real, and their execution status is labeled accurately.
